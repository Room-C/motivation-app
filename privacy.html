Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 3, 2026

Daily Lift is a local-first quote app. This policy explains what information the app uses, what stays on your device, and how to contact us.

Information Daily Lift uses

Daily Lift may use the following information to operate the app:

  • Onboarding choices, selected tags, reminder preferences, favorites, and theme settings.
  • An anonymous installation ID used to support app configuration, generation batches, analytics, and deletion requests.
  • Basic product analytics such as quote impressions, likes, copies, downloads, skips, and app version.
  • Subscription status from RevenueCat and the App Store or Google Play. We do not receive your full payment card details.

Local-first behavior

Core app data such as favorites, current quote position, themes, reminder preferences, and fallback quote queues are designed to work locally. Daily Lift does not require an account for the MVP.

What is not uploaded by default

The app is designed not to upload free-text struggle notes by default. If that changes, the app experience and this policy should be updated before the feature is used.

Service providers

Daily Lift may use service providers for subscriptions, analytics, crash reporting, app configuration, anonymous event processing, and quote generation. These providers process information only as needed to provide their services to Daily Lift.

Deletion requests

You can request deletion of anonymous server-side data associated with your installation by contacting contact@dailylift.app. Local data on your device can also be removed by deleting the app.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact contact@dailylift.app.
